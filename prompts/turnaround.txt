Create ONE clean character turnaround sheet for 3D modeling and later rigging, showing four separate full-body views in this order: front, left profile, back, right profile.

Preserve the approved character's identity, outfit, proportions, colors, hairstyle and accessories. Replace any pose in the design reference with a consistent neutral rigging rest pose in ALL four views: an upright, symmetrical T-pose with arms extended horizontally at shoulder height, elbows straight, wrists neutral, hands open and fingers naturally separated. Keep hands and arms clear of the torso. Keep both legs straight and moderately separated, with feet approximately shoulder-width apart and a clearly visible gap between the legs in the front and back views. Do not let thighs, knees, calves or feet touch or fuse. Do not exaggerate the stance, bend the knees, cross the legs or shift weight onto one hip.

Show the SAME pose rotated around the vertical axis, not four independently posed characters. Use orthographic-looking views at the same scale, with matching head height, ground line and body proportions. Keep the entire character, hands and feet inside each view with margins; prevent adjacent views from overlapping. In true profile views, natural overlap of the far-side limbs is acceptable: do not twist the body or invent extra limbs to force a gap.

Make skin, clothing, hair and accessories visually distinct, with clear boundaries wherever visible. Do not redesign or remove clothing to expose hidden anatomy. Use a plain light background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows, no perspective staging, no props and no decorative text. Back details not visible in the source are inferred and require human review. This is a modeling reference sheet, not a posed illustration.
